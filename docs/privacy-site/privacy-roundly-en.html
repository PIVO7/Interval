Privacy Policy — Roundly

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Roundly is an interval timer app for coaches, physiotherapists and anyone who regularly does HIIT/Tabata-style workouts. Roundly collects no personal data: there is no account, no server of our own, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

This document explains what happens to your data and what rights you have.

1. Who we are

Controller (within the meaning of GDPR art. 4(7)):

Jelle Wauters
email: jelle@pivo7.be

2. What data the app uses

a. Favorites

When you save an interval workout as a favorite, the app stores:

  • the name you give it (e.g. "Tabata 30/15"),
  • the length of the work and rest phases (seconds),
  • the number of rounds,
  • the date you created it.

This data lives on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud on your device, iOS syncs it through your own private iCloud storage to your other devices using the same Apple Account. Apple manages that storage; we cannot see it and receive nothing. If iCloud is off, your favorites stay on this device only.

b. In-app purchase (Roundly Pro)

The one-time "Roundly Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We receive no payment details and no Apple ID. Apple keeps track of whether you made the purchase; the app only reads that locally.

c. Local preferences

Only on your device (in iOS' UserDefaults):

  • whether you have seen the onboarding,
  • your settings for signal tones, haptics and voice cues,
  • your chosen appearance (light / dark / system) and workout colours.

d. What we do NOT collect

  • No account, name or email address.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone data.
  • No health data.
  • No analytics, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps or websites.

3. Who data is shared with

No one. The only party that processes anything from the app is Apple:

  • iCloud stores and syncs your favorites in your own iCloud account, if you use iCloud.
  • App Store / StoreKit handles the Roundly Pro purchase.

Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Retention and deletion

You decide: delete a favorite in the app and it disappears from your other devices too. Delete the app and the local data is gone. Whatever is stored in iCloud can be erased via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Roundly.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because we keep no data about you, in practice you exercise these rights directly in the app and in your iCloud settings. You can always email questions to jelle@pivo7.be. You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or the supervisory authority in your own EU country.

6. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we update the date at the top. Earlier versions remain available through the version history.

7. Contact

Questions? Email jelle@pivo7.be.

Short version: Roundly collects nothing. Your favorites live on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. No account, no server, no analytics.
